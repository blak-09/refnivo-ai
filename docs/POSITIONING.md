# Refnivo — positioning

## What we call it

**Refnivo is a creator commerce platform for Indian product brands.**

> Grow with creators. Pay only for results.

Elevator line: Refnivo helps product brands grow through creators and customers. Brands run affiliate and referral
campaigns that pay only on verified sales, commission UGC at a fixed fee, and manage every creator partnership in one place.

## Why "creator commerce" (and not "referral & affiliate platform")

1. **The old label describes one product out of three.** "Referral & affiliate" covers campaigns, but not UGC (paying
   for content) or Partners (managing relationships). A brand reading the old hero would not know we do UGC.
2. **It names the outcome the buyer wants.** D2C founders and marketers buy *sales and content from creators*, not
   "infrastructure". "Creator commerce" ties creators to commerce in two words and is a category they already search for.
3. **It still includes customers.** Customer referrals are the same mechanism (link → verified order → reward); customers
   are a brand's smallest creators, so they sit inside the category instead of needing their own.
4. **It leaves room to grow** (more channels, store integrations) without renaming again.

## The product: three ways a brand works with creators

| Product | What the brand gets | How the brand pays the creator | Plan |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Affiliate & referrals** (Sell) | A campaign per product; creators and customers share unique links and QR codes | Commission per **verified** order (a click is never a sale) | Free for 3 launches, then any plan |
| **UGC collaborations** (Create) | Photos and videos made to a brief, with fixed usage rights and up to two revisions | A **fixed fee** per creator, paid directly after the brand approves the content | Starter and Growth |
| **Creator partnerships** (Partner) | Every creator the brand works with, what each one produced, private notes | — (it is the relationship record) | Every plan |

All three sit on one foundation: **verified attribution and a shared record** — links and QR codes, order verification,
a ledger both sides see, audit logs for every change to money, and Razorpay for payments.

## Business model

- **Brands pay a subscription**: Free (3 campaign launches) → Starter ₹999 / 30 days (unlimited launches + UGC) →
  Growth ₹2,499 / 30 days (+ analytics, CSV exports, priority support).
- **Creators and customers never pay.** They earn commission, UGC fees and referral rewards.
- **Refnivo takes no cut of UGC fees**: the brand pays the creator directly and both record it.

## Who it is for

- **Brands (the customer):** Indian D2C and product brands, ~1–50 people, selling online, who already send product to
  creators but cannot prove what it sold or manage it outside chats and spreadsheets.
- **Creators (supply):** nano and micro creators who want clear terms, money they can track, and paid content work.
- **Customers (supply):** happy buyers who share with friends on WhatsApp — no followers needed.

## Why brands choose Refnivo

1. **Pay for results, not reach** — commission only on orders the brand verifies; UGC fees only after approval.
2. **Terms set before the work** — commission, fee, deliverables and usage rights are fixed and visible to both sides.
3. **One shared record** — brands and creators see the same orders, statuses and amounts.
4. **Built for India** — rupee pricing, UPI/Razorpay, WhatsApp and QR sharing, ASCI disclosure reminders.

## Words we use / avoid

- Use: creator commerce, verified sales, fixed fee, usage rights, partners, shared record.
- Avoid: "guaranteed sales", "viral", follower-count promises, real-looking metrics that are not real (label all example
  numbers as examples), and showing creators' self-reported numbers as facts.
